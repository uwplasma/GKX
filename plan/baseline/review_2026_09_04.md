## Companion and literature review

`5a499269` (0.20.0 merge). VMEX's current QA scalar-loss pattern avoids a full
residual Jacobian; GKX's example has drifted from it. ESSOS provides Biot–Savart,
coil parameterization and surface fitting, not a demonstrated GKX-ready

backend; adjacent moment coupling alone does not solve a chaotic adjoint.

Full textbook chapters were not available from publisher previews; request those
for the later detailed collision/spectral derivations rather than claiming to
have read them here.
