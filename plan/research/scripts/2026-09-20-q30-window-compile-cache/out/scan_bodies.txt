The linear cache is not the cause: a probe confirms cache.kperp2, cache.Jl,
cache.JlB and cache.ky are tracers on main when the gradient is taken wrt
rho_star, and main's body is unchanged at 716/783. What changes on the branch
is that grid.ky, grid.dealias_mask and the quadrature weights -- concrete on
the eager route under every parameter -- become arguments, so the expressions
built from them are staged into the body instead of folded on the host before
it is traced. That is the same placement that makes the graph reusable across
geometries.
